**Initial verdict: REVISE; defer a two-contribution CVPR claim, permit only the bounded evidence pilot.** The proposal is honest about its conditional status and substantially more falsifiable than a generic frozen-policy/world-model/scorer combination. It is not currently evidence of two distinct contributions. C1 is an empirical finding that may become substantive; C2 is an application of known value-aware fitting with a different supervision source. Separating them in a contribution list does not itself create independent novelty.

The most defensible unresolved question is whether *measured instruction-conditioned continuation values* systematically reverse an explicitly defined geometric proxy on ordinary held-out CE states, and whether predictable next features help a finite, equally supervised critic resolve the reversals. A consequential, generalizing answer could carry scientific value. The proposal has no CE evidence for opportunity, semantic attribution, predictive usefulness, or affordable deployment.

The strongest objection is compositional prior art plus an attribution gap. [MCR §3.3.2, Eqs. 6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09073v1#S3.SS3.SSS2) already adapts a frozen-encoder predictor with task-ordering loss and a native rollout anchor. Its target is dead-reckoned trajectory deviation, not measured instruction-following return. [World4Scorer Appendix D.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36438v1#A4.SS2) already trains a small outcome scorer over a frozen world model's predictions; its main method jointly uses candidate outcomes and future anchoring, while Appendix C.4 analyzes score differences. These differences leave a narrow empirical opportunity, not an obviously new generic mechanism.

A fresh query also found [Beyond Next-Screen Prediction: The Decision Value of GUI World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24372), submitted 17 October 2026, preprint status. Its primary abstract already describes matched direct versus prediction-augmented ranking and reports no extra predictive signal in GUI. This does not establish the CE result, but further rules out claiming the generic matched-evaluator test as new. Full text still to inspect. An aggregator suggested v2; direct arXiv metadata lists only v1, and v2 HTML returned 404. Use verified v1.

Before proceeding beyond a pilot, require (1) robust branch opportunity on ordinary held-out roots, (2) an explicit operational definition of geometric ordering and semantic disagreement, (3) positive actual-then-predicted feature benefit over same-label full-history direct critics and tiny predictors, (4) benefit from measured labels beyond weighted native prediction and close ranking/value-aware rivals, (5) complete closed-loop and hardware costs. An image-feature predictor does not create information beyond its inputs; any finite-model benefit must earn its added computation and pretraining/data budget.

Plausible salvage is an unusually careful CE finding plus a minimal, effective adaptation that has a demonstrated reason to exist. A learned scorer ignoring future inputs, an expensive predictor matched by a tiny/direct model, mismatch driven only by metric/reference artifacts, or loss improvement through critic exploitation would defeat that story. In that event, preserve the negative diagnostic result if generalizable, but do not relabel two ablations as two contributions.
