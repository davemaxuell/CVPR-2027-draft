## 1. What the closest precedents actually establish

The novelty objection is compositional, not an assertion that one paper implements every CE detail. The exact combination of instruction/history, four primitive branches, a fixed reference continuation and a frozen critic is not identical to the inspected papers. Absence of that exact combination does not establish a nonroutine contribution.

## 2. C1 and C2: distinct claims, currently not two established contributions

**C1 could stand on its own only as a substantive finding.** Its value would be an explanation of where ordinary CE decisions fail, how often, what loss they cause, and when predicted visual consequences resolve them beyond equally informed direct evaluation. A protocol, a curated set of obvious turn examples, or a positive permutation ablation is not enough. C1 could remain useful if C2 fails, provided its findings generalize and identify a consequential failure rather than an expected metric difference.

**C2 is currently a routine application candidate with a narrow empirical opportunity.** The objective is native prediction plus centered utility-residual regression. It changes the supervision semantics, not the general optimization principle. Its potential contribution depends on a demonstrated failure of simpler existing objectives and evidence that the particular correction solves C1. If it improves aggregate CE scores without establishing that link, there may be a useful engineering result, but the proposed two-part scientific argument has not been demonstrated.

The pair is therefore not inherently an artificial split: a nonobvious empirical finding and a causal remedy can be distinct claims. **At present, however, splitting an unmeasured application hypothesis and its standard value-aware intervention into C1/C2 does not meet the requested threshold.** There are no results establishing either side, and the generic ideas are heavily anticipated. The honest current description is one conditional research direction with two evidence obligations.

The strongest remaining objection is this: **why should a costly learned future representation, trained to fit the same four outcome values, outperform a well-trained direct value model or simple task-weighted native predictor at equal data and total compute?** A deterministic predictor is a function of its legal history/action inputs. Conditional on those inputs and fixed learned weights, it cannot supply new observations about this particular environment state. Its possible advantage is useful representation, prior knowledge or computation for finite models. That is a legitimate hypothesis, but it needs a strong direct control with the same history, permissible training information and budget. Conversely, this information argument does not prove a finite predictor pipeline can never win.

## 3. Semantic attribution remains a demanding claim

V1's semantic/geometric wording was too easy to satisfy with a weak proxy. The outcome target contains success, geometric whole-path nDTW, and costs. Disagreement with immediate goal distance can arise from delayed progress, turn/STOP handling, reference-route choice, collision mechanics or later actor failure. It does not by itself demonstrate semantic understanding.

V2 §7 corrects the major issues: it specifies privileged immediate geodesic progress, signs and units, excludes geometric ties from strict reversals, reports STOP separately, and distinguishes this diagnostic from MCR's trajectory-deviation target. It requires independent route-stage annotations and ordinary-root prevalence before semantic wording. These changes make the study reviewable.

The residual limitation is that **Q_ref evaluates the chosen action followed by a particular actor**, not the intrinsic instruction correctness of that first action or optimal semantic value. Changing an instruction or its history can change the actor's later competence as well as the task. Even a validated Q reversal under matched physical conditions needs interpretation. The semantic subset should mark immediate action/route-stage compliance and localize later continuation failures, with annotators blind to model scores and test returns. Report these alongside the separate utility components. If only the policy-relative outcome flips are established, call them that.

For C1 to be convincing, require:

1. A prespecified, ordinary held-out root sample with scene/episode denominators, strict-reversal coverage, geometry ties, uncertain utility pairs and STOP strata. Report both prevalence and consequence; a high rate among a tiny selected subset is not an overall finding.
2. A second, appropriately route-aware or MCR-style comparator wherever a superiority claim concerns trajectory geometry. The initial goal-progress diagnostic cannot defeat MCR, and abundant LEFT/RIGHT ties cannot be counted as reversals. A fair H1 port may not exist; report that limit.
3. Independent valid language/stage annotations or matched valid tasks supporting the semantic interpretation. Do not equate arbitrary instruction deletion/shuffling with a valid new task. Keep curated stress cases separate from ordinary prevalence.
4. Evidence that predictable features resolve the nontrivial disagreement after controlling direct history/value learning. An actual-next observation can reveal previously hidden information; a predicted-next feature cannot be assumed to do so.

## 4. Mechanism and experiment audit

The centered loss is mathematically coherent. For residual vector e=s−y, all-pairs squared gaps equal K times ||Pe||². The root-common offset is deliberately removed. The displayed regret inequality is a correct conditional algebraic observation when y contains true action values; it is not a learned-model guarantee. Neither the identity nor inequality is a new contribution. STOP supplies no dynamics gradient, but its residual affects the centered movement gradients, as A7 now states.

The supplied scorer's `future_patches.detach()` blocks the proposed adaptation, while its baseline behavior is appropriate for frozen-world-model scorer fitting. An opt-in differentiable input path with frozen scorer parameters is needed. Native feature anchoring can discourage large distortion but cannot certify truthful predicted features. A loss decrease produced by moving features to a critic's erroneous region would fail the mechanism claim, even with all parameters frozen except dynamics.

A7's completed E0–E5 sequence is a sound falsification ladder. Its replay/cache/metric checks, independent stochastic selection/evaluation seeds, matching of legal history, actual-next qualification, retrained null/copy controls, cheap predictor, distillation rival, held-out complete episodes and full-cost measurements address the main confounders. Its numeric utility/SR/latency gates are explicit planning assumptions, not empirically established thresholds or universal acceptance standards. Actual-next failure with an underfit critic is inconclusive about all predictive approaches; a precise direct-value tie defeats the current deployment justification.

The cheapest credible order remains:

| Stage | Evidence bought | Decision if it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Reliable reference actor and replay; then branch opportunity | Valid labels and enough actionable headroom | Fix invalid integration; if precise opportunity is too small, stop this H1 candidate design. |
| Actual-next versus same-label/full-history direct value | Whether next observations offer a usable signal for this critic | Diagnose fitting first; do not expand expensive prediction on an unqualified critic. |
| Native predicted features versus direct/copy/tiny-predictor and matched-cost alternatives | Whether the predictor contributes useful finite-model representation | Remove online prediction if a simpler adequate control matches it. |
| One centered objective versus native, per-action value loss, simple feature weighting, observed-feature contrast and relevant value/ranking rivals | Whether measured utility-gap fitting adds something beyond ordinary alternatives | Prefer the simpler winner; do not call two equivalent loss parameterizations two baselines or contributions. |
| Paired unseen closed loop and full hardware profile | Whether offline corrections survive repeated action selection and real cost | Narrow or stop the deployment claim if gains disappear, become harmful, or exceed equal-cost direct alternatives. |

I agree with retaining A7's cheaper observed-feature contrast as a mandatory substantive rival before a methodological claim, even when overt critic exploitation is not observed. It should not be added as a second proposed method merely to inflate contributions. The initial null/copy/tiny-predictor gates should eliminate unnecessary work before every close-loss variant is trained.

V2 also correctly admits B→frozen critic→phi supervision. The adapted WM has indirectly used B labels, so it no longer has strict A-only information even if its explicit adaptation batches are A. That is legitimate training use if counted and matched, with C/unseen protected. The union-of-history control similarly prevents extra real observations in the WM from masquerading as prediction benefit. I reread the corrected experiment register: E4 now names the centered objective and preserves A7's rival preference.

## 5. Viability, scientific value and resources

**Scientific value: plausible but unproved.** The central problem matters if the measured candidate opportunity is substantial and the learned prediction channel corrects a failure that direct context cannot resolve efficiently. A clean negative result could also inform CE model design, but only with adequate fitting, uncertainty and generalization. An underpowered debug run or one actor's brittle continuation behavior would not support a broad negative claim.

**Engineering viability: not yet established, not disproved.** A6's hardware/environment records show an 8,188 MiB RTX4070 Laptop GPU and scoped CPU-only Torch without Habitat/Habitat-Sim in the inspected interpreter. Those records do not prove no other setup exists. Usable CE assets, trained navigation adapters, selected transfer weights and full CUDA integration have not been demonstrated. Original source code, tensor checks and parameter arithmetic cannot substitute for them. [A6 evidence](06_feasibility.md), [hardware](../experiments/06/hardware.json), [Python probe](../experiments/06/python313_probe.json).

The 14×14 DINO default versus 16×16 scorer mismatch is material: original code hardcodes 256 raw patches and 64 pooled patches; A6 traced a default 196-pixel encoder path producing 196 patches. V2's conditional common 14×14→7×7 adapter is sensible, but no chosen checkpoint has validated it. Native preprocessing/grid, actual and predicted sources, and CDiT decode/re-encode must be identical within the intended comparisons. This is an interface repair, not a scientific contribution.

The expensive asset is the branch dataset, not just the 3M-parameter scorer. Each movement action invokes a potentially long reference-policy continuation; prefix replay and stochastic repeats multiply cost. A7 correctly treats 64 roots/eight episodes as debugging and places collection caps before expansion. Its branch-step bound is a planning bound, not measured throughput. Offline staging can reduce simultaneous residency, but online inference still includes actor/vision/KV history, simulator graphics, all futures, scorer and any transfers. CDiT additionally brings sampling and decode/re-encode costs. No full 8GB pipeline result exists.

Preserving the locked SmolVLM2-500M, OneVision-0.5B and Qwen3-VL-2B trials, and the transferred/fresh DINO and CDiT families, while starting with one policy and the deterministic DINO pair is appropriate. It honors the registry without demanding all twelve combinations before the mechanism earns further work. One positive actor result must remain actor-conditional until replication. A failed 8GB configuration would narrow the lightweight claim, not prove the scientific question false.

V2 contains a few inherited short labels such as “C1 Semantic ordering” in §4 and “semantic/geometric disagreement” in §3. I read these under the explicit restrictions in §7; any later paper should use the narrow outcome/proxy wording until semantic evidence exists. This is a wording cleanup, not an additional mechanism requirement.

**Evidence that would change this recommendation:** reproducible useful primitive opportunity; a consequential, nontrivial mismatch with independently validated interpretation; predicted-feature benefit beyond strong direct/history and cheap alternatives; differential benefit of measured-return adaptation over close simpler losses; unseen closed-loop gains at acceptable total cost. These findings would make the two-claim structure defensible, although venue acceptance is never guaranteed by a checklist.

**Reason to stop this mechanism:** precise lack of opportunity, no useful predictable channel after adequate fitting, metric/continuation artifacts explaining the claimed semantic effect, a simpler equal-budget model matching the result, critic-exploitation-only gains, or an unfavorable complete accuracy–cost comparison. Invalid or underpowered experiments mean repair/inconclusive, not rejection by nonsignificance.

**Disposition after frozen v2: REVISE / bounded qualification work only.** Its description and controls are substantially improved. The strongest objection—routine value-aware application without demonstrated need or a distinct scientific finding—remains unresolved by text. The project should buy that evidence before committing to the full registry or claiming two CVPR contributions.
