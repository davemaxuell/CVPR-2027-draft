# VLN research investigation

**Cutoff: 22 October 2026. Complete within documented evidence and access limits.**

This package covers 131 papers, one internal report and all 157 supplied PDFs. Ten delegated assignments reviewed the original material, traced subsequent research, developed proposals and independently challenged their logic, novelty and feasibility. The maximum concurrency was the coordinator plus three delegates.

Start with [the main report](Research_Report.md), then [the ranked recommendation](proposals/Final_Recommendation.md). Three conditional studies remain: recovery labels, route validity and negative semantic memory. Their broad mechanisms have close prior art; none has an established navigation gain. Ordered future-event prediction was rejected as an original-method proposal.

No learned navigation benchmark, model training or simulator rollout was run. Executed evidence consists of finite mathematical checks, static geometry analysis and isolated source-function tests. Missing assets/code and partial review scopes are documented in the linked records. The final recommendation starts with an independently annotated diagnostic, not an assumed performance improvement.
