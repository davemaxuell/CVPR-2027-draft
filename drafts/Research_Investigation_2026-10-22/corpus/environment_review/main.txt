Environment understanding for VLMs and VLAs (2020–2025) Environment understanding has become the binding constraint on Visual Language Action models, not model size. Across roughly 70 papers from 2020–2025, three failure modes recur: VLMs are "near-blind" on orientation and metric distance (BLINK, OpenEQA, VSR, What's Up, RoboSpatial); current VLAs ground objects semantically but place them poorly in 3D (SpatialVLA, 3D-VLA, DP3); and dynamic state-tracking over long horizons remains an open problem (EgoSchema, OpenEQA, EmbodiedBench). Three convergent solution strategies have emerged in 2024–2025 — synthetic spatial-VQA data scaling (SpatialVLM, SpatialRGPT, RoboSpatial), lightweight 3D-positional injection into 2D VLMs (LLaVA-3D, SpatialVLA's Ego3D PE, SpatialBot's depth API), and pointing/keypoint outputs as the bridge from VLM to action (Molmo, RoboPoint, MOKA, PIVOT). On the efficiency side, OpenVLA-OFT, SmolVLA, MiniVLA, RoboMamba, DeeR-VLA and VLA-Cache demonstrate that 10–40× speedups are obtainable with parity-or-better task success — but each requires explicit grounding cues (FiLM, action chunks, depth, traces) to retain perception quality. For a researcher working on efficient ML and Korean embodied AI, the gaps are unusually well-defined: no major paper in this set evaluates non-English temporal or spatial grounding for embodied agents, and Korean robot- language datasets effectively do not exist outside of translated LLaVA splits. The report below organizes 70+ papers into the user's six requested themes, with 🪶 flags for efficiency-relevant work and 🌐 flags for multilingual/Korean-relevant work. 1. Semantic scene graphs and object relations Two architectural patterns dominate: 3D scene graphs serialized for LLM planners (SayPlan, ConceptGraphs, HOV-SG, BBQ, Clio, GraphEQA, 3DGraphLLM) and open-vocabulary 2D/3D scene-graph generation models (Open3DSG, OpenPSG, PGSG, VLPrompt). A third strand binds affordances to scene structure (AffordanceLLM, ManipLLM, RoboPoint, ManipVQA), turning relations into actionable edges. 3D scene graphs for embodied planning ConceptGraphs (Gu et al., ICRA 2024) builds open-vocabulary 3D scene graphs from posed RGB-D streams using SAM segmentation, CLIP/DINO embeddings, and an LLM that infers inter- object edges. The graph is text-serializable for GPT-4 task planning and was demoed on Spot/Jackal robots for traversability reasoning. SayPlan (Rana et al., CoRL 2023) 🪶 is the canonical hierarchical 3DSG planner: floors → rooms → assets → objects, encoded as JSON for GPT-4. Three innovations — semantic search via expand/contract API, integration of a Dijkstra path planner, and iterative scene-graph-simulator feedback — give 73.3% long-horizon correctness vs. 33.3% baselines while reducing input tokens by 60–82% through hierarchy collapse. SOTA on AGD20K with strong Internet-image generalization. ManipLLM (Li et al., CVPR 2024) chains object identification → pixel affordance → object-centric pose, with active impedance and test-time adaptation; strong cross-category generalization on SAPIEN. RoboPoint (Yuan et al., CoRL 2024) instruction-tunes a VLM on procedurally generated 3D-scene synthetic data to predict 2D keypoints that lift to 3D via depth; +21.8% over GPT-4o on spatial-affordance accuracy and +30.5% on downstream task success vs. PIVOT. 3. Spatial reasoning and 3D scene understanding The 2024–2025 wave converges on three complementary approaches: synthetic spatial-VQA scaling, lightweight 3D-positional injection into 2D VLMs, and pointing/keypoint outputs as VLM-to-action bridges. Spatial VLMs (2D inputs) SpatialVLM (Chen et al., CVPR 2024) 🪶 lifts open Internet images into metric 3D and generates 2B spatial-VQA examples on 10M images; beats GPT-4V on quantitative distance with no 3D backbone, demonstrably useful as a dense reward annotator. SpatialRGPT (Cheng et al., NeurIPS 2024) is region-level, with an automated 3D-scene-graph data pipeline (8.7M concepts, 5M regions) and a flexible Depth-Anything-V2 plugin; introduces SpatialRGPT- Bench with metric ground truth across indoor/outdoor/sim. SpatialBot (Cai et al., ICRA 2025) ingests paired RGB+depth with a unified uint24 depth encoding (1mm–131m) and a depth-API tool call; releases SpatialQA, SpatialQA-E, and SpatialBench. SpatialPIN (Ma et al., NeurIPS 2024) 🪶 is training-free: progressively prompts a frozen VLM with outputs from off-the-shelf 3D foundation models. SAT (Ray et al., COLM 2025) trains on dynamic spatial reasoning (perspective taking, ego-motion, action consequences) from ProcTHOR sims, lifting LLaVA-13B by +23% on CV-Bench, +18% on VSR, +8% on VSI-Bench. RoboSpatial (Song et al., CVPR 2025 Oral) is the most production-impactful: 1M images, 5K 3D scans, 3M spatial relations with ego-/world-/object-centric reference frames. Fine-tuning lifts VILA from 40.2→63.9%, RoboPoint context 41.4→68.9%, and tabletop manipulation 23.7→52.6%. Adopted by NVIDIA GR00T N1.5, Qwen3-VL, Gemini Robotics. Molmo (Deitke et al., CVPR 2025 Best Paper Honorable Mention) uses PixMo-Points (2.3M human-annotated question-point pairs) to teach the model to literally point at referenced objects; Molmo-72B is the first fully-open SoTA VLM without distillation and the foundation for Molmo-Act. Pointing and keypoint approaches RoboPoint (Yuan et al., CoRL 2024) trains a VLM to emit image keypoints as text tokens, lifting to 3D via depth; releases the Where2Place benchmark. PIVOT (Nasiriany et al., ICML 2024) 🪶 casts continuous spatial decisions as iterative VQA over numbered visual proposals — frozen VLM, no training. MOKA (Liu et al., RSS 2024) 🪶 uses mark-based visual prompting (grids + labeled candidate keypoints) with GPT-4V for zero-shot manipulation. 3D-aware VLMs (native 3D backbones) 3D-LLM (Hong et al., NeurIPS 2023) is the first LLM family to ingest 3D point clouds, extracting features via back-projected multi-view 2D CLIP/BLIP. LEO (Huang et al., ICML 2024) unifies egocentric 2D + object-centric 3D point clouds + text in one autoregressive sequence and is trained for vision-language-action generalist behavior; SOTA on ScanQA, SQA3D, Scan2Cap, ObjNav. 3D-VisTA (Zhu et al., ICCV 2023) showed simple SSL pretraining on ScanScribe (278K scene-text pairs) suffices without task-specific heads. Action representation is half the efficiency story. OpenVLA-OFT's switch from 256-bin discrete to continuous L1 + chunking (K=8) gives 26× speedup AND raises LIBERO from 76.5%→97.1%. FAST tokenization (Pertsch et al., RSS 2025) compresses action chunks via DCT+BPE, producing 10× fewer tokens; combined with π₀ it matches diffusion quality with 5× less training. MiniVLA's RVQ chunks alone push LIBERO-90 from 62%→82% with no perception change. Explicit grounding cues beat scale alone. TraceVLA (visual trajectory overlays), ECoT (bbox + gripper coords in CoT), and FiLM in OpenVLA-OFT+ each yield 10–30% absolute gains over the same backbone — strong evidence that the bottleneck for novel-scene action quality is grounding, not VLM size. Efficiency need not cost grounding. EfficientVLA explicitly shows pruning 36% of CogACT's parameters raises pick-coke-can success from 91.3%→94.0%, indicating VLAs are heavily over- parameterized. SmolVLA, MiniVLA, RoboMamba, OpenVLA-OFT, and DeeR-VLA all preserve or improve task accuracy while being substantially smaller/faster. Efficient cousins worth tracking for the user: NORA (3B Qwen2.5-VL based), Helix (Figure dual-system 7B+80M for humanoid 200 Hz control, blog only), Gemini Robotics-ER (3D spatial reasoning specialist), HPT (modular stem-trunk-head, NeurIPS 2024), and CrossFormer (CoRL 2024 oral; one transformer, 4 action spaces, no observation alignment). 6. Research gaps and open problems Aggregating across all surveys (Ma et al. 2024, Firoozi et al. 2024, Xiao et al. 2024, Gao et al. 2024, Zhu et al. Neurocomputing 2024, the LLM-Spatial-Intelligence Survey 2025, and the Spatial Intelligence in VLMs Survey 2025) and benchmark failure modes (BLINK, OpenEQA, VSR, EmbodiedBench, VLABench, RoboSpatial, MV-RoboBench), eight gaps are consistently identified: Perception-side gaps: VLMs are near-blind on orientation, viewpoint, and metric distance — VSR shows orientation relations are at chance, OpenEQA shows visual context barely helps spatial questions, BLINK shows GPT-4V is 13% above random. Reference-frame reasoning (ego-/world-/object-centric) is barely addressed pre- RoboSpatial. Multi-view and perspective-taking spatial reasoning remain unsolved (3DSRBench, MV- RoboBench, All-Angles Bench). Long-tailed predicate distribution and biased "trivial" relations (on, has) plague scene- graph generation. Action-side gaps: VLAs misjudge depth/relative position when placing objects ("place X next to Y") — concentrated failure mode across OpenVLA, π₀, RDT-1B, TinyVLA, NORA. Cross-embodiment zero-shot transfer remains the open frontier; RoboGate independent eval shows zero-shot near 0% across multiple efficient VLAs. Long-horizon multi-step reasoning (RefSpatial-Bench, EmbodiedEval, VLABench) is still substantially below human. Sim-to-real correlation in VLABench shows pretrained VLAs do not exhibit the expected emergent generalization. Infrastructure gaps: LIBERO numbers across papers are not directly comparable (different fine-tuning recipes, image preprocessing). Standardized multi-dimensional embodied evaluation is fragmented; Embodied Arena and EmbodiedBench attempt unification but have not yet displaced ad-hoc evals. Safety, uncertainty quantification, and real-time inference are central in robotics- foundation-model surveys but under-evaluated in current benchmarks. Multilingual/cultural gap (especially salient for the user): xGQA documents ~38pp cross-lingual accuracy drop; no major paper in this set evaluates non-English temporal or spatial grounding for embodied agents. No Korean-language robot-instruction dataset for manipulation/navigation exists publicly. Korean VLMs (HyperCLOVA-X, VARCO-VISION) ship Korean grounding but have not been integrated into embodied benchmarks. Conclusion The 2020–2025 literature shows environment understanding is now the limiting factor on VLA performance, and the field has settled on a clear architectural pattern: lightweight 3D-positional encoding on 2D VLM patches, paired with action representations (chunks, FAST tokens, flow matching) that decouple action throughput from perception. For an efficient-ML researcher, the empirical evidence is unusually clean — OpenVLA-OFT (97.1% LIBERO at 109.7 Hz), SmolVLA (450M, 18ms/step), MiniVLA (1B, LIBERO-90 82%), RoboMamba (3× speedup via SSM), and EfficientVLA (71% FLOPs reduction with no accuracy drop) together demonstrate that 10–40× efficiency gains are routinely achievable while preserving or improving task success, provided explicit grounding cues (FiLM, action chunks, depth, traces, affordance points) are retained. Three asymmetries worth exploiting: (i) action representation upgrades give larger gains than perception upgrades on current benchmarks; (ii) lightweight 3D-positional injection beats heavy point-cloud encoders on most indoor/embodied tasks; and (iii) the multilingual and Korean embodied-AI corner is genuinely empty — no major paper in this set evaluates non- English embodied grounding, no Korean robot-instruction dataset exists, and the closest production systems (HyperCLOVA-X, VARCO-VISION, RoboSpatial) have not been combined. A Korean SmolVLA-style model trained on a Korean equivalent of LIBERO/RxR with RoboSpatial- style ego/world/object-centric annotations would address gaps that every survey above explicitly flags.
