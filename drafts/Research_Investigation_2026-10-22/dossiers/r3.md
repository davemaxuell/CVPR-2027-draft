## Problem, motivation, and operating assumptions

Use a trained fast VLN expert until anomaly, then powerful GPT-4o correction. It is not entirely training-free: GridMM runner and GNN regulator are trained. Discrete graph/pose/depth given; plans restart from initial location in some cases.

## Method, training, inference, and equations

Methodology: Runner GridMM~160 M stores projected RGBD features in egocentric grid and uses cross-modal transformer/ FFN . Regulator checks max node visits>4, trajectory length>20, or GNN anomaly score>.35; two edge-aware graph attention layers use position, last visit, visual embedding. Train pseudo-labels from train and val-seen trajectories: nominal if episode ultimately succeeds OR all past nodes lie on GT path; otherwise anomaly. Future outcome is privileged label not causal leak at inference. Exact supervised loss not defined in reviewed main text. Stop always verified by GPT-4o with instruction/panorama. On switch GPT-4o Ruminator takes over for the rest of episode(irreversible), shared memory, three prompts perception→long-term plan→candidate action. Critical formulation may restart from start and reset memory; return travel cost must be counted for same SPL protocol. LLM is frozen; runner has inherited VLN training, scoring model additional training. Lack of regulator equations/threshold calibration splits limits reproducibility.

## Novelty and closest predecessors

Anomaly-triggered expert/ VLM handoff and trained graph failure predictor; MapGPT already graph plans, early dual process is not generic novelty now given AdaNav / AwareVLN . R3's handoff is one-way, not per step bidirectional selective thinking.
