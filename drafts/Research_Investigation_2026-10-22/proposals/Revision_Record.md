## Remaining objections and next evidence

P1 still needs consequential natural errors, reliable obligations and observable task state. P2 still needs a fixed pool, complete route evidence and incremental selection value beyond established filters. P4 still needs an active, naturally harmful write path and identifying evidence that beats quarantine. All three can be rejected by their first audits. The proposed finite-state and scoring constructions do not prove learned-policy generalization.

No new benchmark, model training, natural annotation collection or source execution was performed during this revision. Assignment 07 directly revisited scoped methods in LAW pp 3–4, CVS pp 3–5, Noisy RM pp 5–6 and HiMeco pp 5–6; the remaining new-source/code findings are attributed to the independent reviewers. The original full-corpus synthesis and its access limits remain unchanged. Additional review scope and input hashes are in [the revision manifest](../evidence/07/revision_scope.json).

The three projects retain A–J details, falsification gates and practical prerequisites. The initial order P1 → P2 → P4 reflects the cheapest informative evidence, not a promise of three inventions. The [focused recheck](../evaluations/08_revision_check.md) found no material logical blocker under the stated assumptions. The coordinator accepted the diagnostic ranking and explicit limits. Its sole subsequent formulation clarification states zero STOP cost, zero accepting-terminal value and infinite failed-terminal value, with absorbing terminals. The [acceptance record](../evidence/coordinator_acceptance.json) preserves before/after hashes and the scope of these edits.
