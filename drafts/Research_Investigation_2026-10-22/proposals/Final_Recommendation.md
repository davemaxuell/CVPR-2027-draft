# Recommendation and decision gates

**Start with the recovery-label audit (P1), before building a new architecture.** The investigation supports three conditional research projects, not three established new methods. Independent review found close prior art for the generic mechanisms in all four drafts. P3's ordered-future predictor is rejected as an original-method proposal; its representational diagnostic remains useful. The retained projects must earn a stronger contribution through the experiments below.

## Ranking

| Priority | Project | Why this position | Novelty and logical status | Empirical status and feasibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **P1 — Recovery labels** | A tightly specified teacher/continuation audit can test the premise before expensive training. The base code is inspectable and annotation can separate geometric efficiency from actual instruction completion. | Product-state planning, partial-label loss, language-aligned teachers and history-to-task-state estimation are established. A new contribution would be independent recovery-label evidence and a controlled intervention beyond LAW and reward-machine baselines. The corrected oracle is valid only under its declared graph, event and action contract. | Toy graphs and isolated DUET source checks support possibility; no natural error rate or learned gain is measured. Requires visual traces, a verified checkpoint/preprocessing stack and annotation. |
| 2 | **P2 — Route validity** | A fixed-pool audit can reveal whether success filtering targets a different property from route-language validity. It has a clear label-only intervention with a common DUET trainer. | Generic validity filtering plus hard-positive selection is already in CVS and related data-selection work. A witness-grounded route audit may be a useful empirical contribution; a standard alignment verifier is a strong baseline. The selector is well defined, but neither attention nor a sigmoid certifies truth. | No candidate-pool audit, validity model or navigator training has run. Needs the fixed generated pool, route images, frozen difficulty scores and independent labels, with annotation time and optimizer exposure controlled. |
| 3 | **P4 — Negative memory** | An actual conditional write/read pathway is verified, but real prevalence and the value beyond simple quarantine remain unknown. Natural repeated-episode traces and reliable failure labels are more demanding. | Diagnosis before memory revision is already in HiMeco, EmbodiSkill and DoCtOR. Only a narrower claim-specific negative-write study with deployable evidence remains a candidate. Identifiability and joint gate precision require data, not just separate loss heads. | Extracted release functions demonstrate a conditional harmful-write case; they do not establish the paper EEC's behavior or a measured navigation effect. Begin with log/replay diagnostics; a learned method must beat freeze/quarantine and witness-only controls. |
| Rejected as a method proposal | **P3 — Joint future events** | A correct symbolic distinction is insufficient novelty. Generalized value functions and predictive-state representations already express such ordered predictive questions. | The stronger matched-marginal counterexample is valid for its event features. It does not establish actual NavQ target collisions, predictable hidden information or a new world-model principle. | Retain as an optional representation audit. Defer Q-model training and a route-task port unless a distinct learnability/cost result emerges. |

These priorities use evidence strength, scientific importance, distinctness from prior art, experiment cost and falsifiability. They are not numerical forecasts of acceptance or performance. P1 and P2 currently have a stronger **diagnostic-study case** than an architectural novelty case. P4 is conditional on natural harmful writes and on an improvement beyond conservative memory policies. None is ready to be described as a validated CVPR contribution.

## Next decisive experiment

Use the [initial experiment plan](../experiments/Initial_Experiment_Plan.md) and [empty annotation template](../experiments/recovery_annotation_template.csv). First obtain a verified frozen-policy trace set with actual visual evidence. The proposed pilot is 200 natural recovery checkpoints plus 100 separately constructed paired histories, with two blinded annotators and adjudication; these are planning quantities, not collected data or a power calculation.

For each interpretable checkpoint, evaluate complete teacher continuations including STOP. Record instruction completion separately from cost-optimal action agreement. A harmless detour must not be labeled a semantic failure. Begin with a common primitive-neighbor-plus-STOP interface; later native DUET comparisons must also match map-dependent candidates, endpoint termination, macro-route observations and cached return. Compare geometric/nDTW, LAW-style waypoint, reversible milestone/reward-machine and proposed independently grounded teachers under the same information and labels.

The first **go** result would be reliable, non-negligible, scene-distributed natural disagreement that remains after legitimate alternatives and strong teachers are considered. Its magnitude and confidence must come from the data. A **no-go** result is equally useful: negligible incidence, ambiguous semantics, or a simple existing teacher removing the gap means there is no basis for the proposed new method. Only after this gate should identical policy architectures be trained on the competing labels. Additional state modules are a later, separately controlled experiment.

The current workspace supports static graph and source checks. It does not yet support the natural-trace experiment: visual assets/features, usable checkpoint, tokenized annotations and simulator setup are unestablished. The physical 8 GB-class GPU is present, but installed PyTorch is CPU-only. These are concrete prerequisites, not measured training-time or memory estimates. [Independent feasibility and asset audit](../evaluations/10_feasibility.md).

## Evidence that would change the ranking

- **Move away from P1** if LAW/reversible progress already handles the natural cases, or if strict procedural requirements are too rare or ambiguous to support a meaningful study.
- **Move P2 first** if a representative pool shows a substantial validity/competence mismatch and witness labels improve selection beyond CVS-style and ordinary compatibility controls at equal human effort.
- **Move P4 upward** if natural traces show frequent, consequential semantic contamination that freeze/quarantine cannot fix, with identifying evidence available at deployment and no representation or feedback advantage for the proposed arm.
- **Reconsider P3 only** if a specific result beyond established predictive questions is demonstrated—for example a verified accuracy/cost or learnability distinction against matched GVF, sequence and future-QA alternatives. The symbolic counterexample alone is insufficient.
