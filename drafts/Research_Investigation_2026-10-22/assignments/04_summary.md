# Assignment 04: language, uncertainty, adaptation and embodied evidence

Cutoff: **22 October 2026**. This assignment reviewed 32 supplied papers and one internal report, producing 33 dossiers and 51 scoped bottleneck records. All records are saved in [04_results.json](04_results.json). Each dossier separates paper experiments, inspected source, deductions and access limits. This is a completed source investigation within the documented limits; it is **not** a reproduction of navigation benchmarks.

The useful distinction across these papers is what evidence a method can actually access. A pretrained navigator, an online learner receiving episode outcomes, an agent asking a person for help, an interactive QA system, and a model inspecting a recorded video solve different decision problems. Their success rates cannot be pooled into one architecture ranking. Likewise, “training-free” can describe a pruning module or inference procedure while the underlying navigation policy remains trained; “CE” should be written as either **continuous environments** or **cross-entropy loss** to avoid conflating task and objective.

## Findings that should shape the proposals

**Grounding and progress are established; verifying a full ordered obligation remains harder.** [FGR2R](../dossiers/fgr2r.md) supplies intermediate instruction-to-path alignment and a learned shift mechanism. [GELA](../dossiers/gela.md) adds entity/landmark grounding supervision; [Landmark-RxR](../dossiers/landmark_rxr.md) supplies human chunk alignment and landmark rewards. [M-Track](../dossiers/mtrack.md) explicitly gates subtask completion, and its limitations already identify procedural instructions requiring action history. An ordered-obligation proposal must compare with these mechanisms, not merely with endpoint SR. M-Track's incomplete public runtime and FGR2R's data/chunking-only release are practical reproduction limits, not permission to omit the papers as prior art. [NavNuances](../dossiers/navnuances.md) is useful diagnostic evidence, but some released landmark metrics assess start/end geometry rather than every intermediate action. Root's separate geometry audit and assignment 05's metric audit provide additional scope checks.

**A mismatch does not identify its cause.** [Mind the Error](../dossiers/mind_error.md) detects and localizes instruction corruption conditioned on a collected trajectory, while [ULN](../dossiers/uln.md) studies missing information. Neither automatically tells an agent whether a conflict was caused by an erroneous instruction, missed perception, wrong route or execution failure. [AIUTA](../dossiers/aiuta.md) can ask a user who sees the target image; [IEVE](../dossiers/ieve.md) uses distance/match verification for instance goals. These are informative alternatives with additional assumptions and costs. A recovery dataset should label language validity independently of a single navigator's competence and retain unresolved perceptual/control causes.

**Success feedback improves adaptation, but its semantics and credit assignment matter.** [FeedTTA](../dossiers/feedtta.md) and [ATENA](../dossiers/atena.md) exploit episode outcome feedback that [FSTTA](../dossiers/fstta.md) does not receive. FeedTTA's hidden-test oracle permits broad category and partial alignment; it should not be treated as a strict route/instance verifier. ATENA reduces external queries with a self-trained outcome head, leaving confident false positives and episode-level blame unresolved. Their mathematical explanations also require narrowing: FeedTTA's main equation and appendix implement different gradient scaling, and ATENA's one-hot mixture does not universally strengthen the parameter gradient. These observations leave their empirical gains intact as reported evidence while motivating equal-feedback-budget controls and independent witness validation.

**Memory and routing address different forms of adaptation.** [GSA-VLN](../dossiers/gsavln.md) persists scene-map evidence across episodes and evaluates instruction-style shift. Its Appendix A.2 describes navigator-based instruction selection and GPT-4o correction during benchmark construction; this is a disclosed construction procedure, not evidence by itself of evaluation-label leakage. [Continual VLN](../dossiers/continualvln.md) learns parameters over domains with stored episodes/logits. [M³E](../dossiers/m3e.md) uses supervised incremental learning and tests held-out episodes within adapted scenes; it does not establish zero-shot adaptation to entirely unobserved scenes. [SAME](../dossiers/same.md) trains a joint expert policy. [slow4fast-VLN](../dossiers/slow4fast.md) claims reflection-driven reusable experience but its inspected release contains unintegrated placeholders. A failure-memory proposal needs to preserve the distinction between stable object identity, an uncertain language interpretation, and a particular controller's inability to reach the object.

**Useful information is not free information.** [Active Visual Information Gathering](../dossiers/activevig.md) improves success at significant travel cost and sometimes changes a correct action into an incorrect one. ULN's physical branches similarly improve SR while reducing SPL in the reviewed setting. [Meta-Explore](../dossiers/metaexplore.md) improves recovery with object spectra, but gains depend on category quality; its corrected REVERIE labels and longer VLN-CE routes are relevant controls. Its physical Jackal demonstration is image-goal navigation, while its language-conditioned continuous-environment result is simulated. A world-model or information-seeking proposal should compare against motion-matched physical lookahead and equal-budget cached evidence, including reorientation and backtracking costs.

**A calibrated answer set is different from calibrated physical success.** [KnowNo](../dossiers/knowno.md) and [Explore until Confident](../dossiers/explore_confident.md) are direct prior art for uncertainty-aware help/stopping. Their coverage statements depend on specified scenario distributions, acceptable answer labels and calibration protocols. Explore until Confident's full appendix proves simultaneous answer inclusion using a minimum-over-time score; it does not guarantee singleton termination, accurate localization or collision-free execution. KnowNo's hardware setup restricts some detection ambiguity and sometimes uses known locations/scripted skills. New claims should name the exact random variable and event being calibrated.

**Textual reasoning and memory do not replace spatial evidence.** [CoT](../dossiers/cot.md) establishes useful prompting effects on selected text tasks, with negative transfers and invalid intermediate rationales in its own analysis. [UoT](../dossiers/uot.md) searches a finite hypothesis set with a simulated truthful answerer, which is not a physical world model. [OmniAgent](../dossiers/omniagent.md) actively queries a recorded audiovisual archive and retains textual summaries, allowing nonphysical temporal access. Its entropy weighting is not a causal credit estimate. [VSI-Bench](../dossiers/vsi_bench.md) and [VLN-MME](../dossiers/vlnmme.md) diagnose spatial/action abilities without establishing full closed-loop navigation success. VSI-Bench's post-answer explanations are interpretive diagnostics, and its cognitive-map benefit varies with model size.

**Embodiment remains an evaluation boundary.** [EmbodiedQA](../dossiers/embodiedqa.md) and [IQA](../dossiers/iqa.md) establish active perception and interaction as older foundations. [Rethinking the Embodied Gap](../dossiers/embodiedgap.md) shows why controllers, camera placement, dynamics and appearance affect transfer. Its 14-episode physical CMA comparison uses a custom stopping threshold and is not RDP robot validation. [Walk and Read Less](../dossiers/walkreadless.md) reduces token computation but does not solve physical travel or uncertainty. The [internal environment review](../dossiers/environment_review.md) is retained as secondary context only.

Assignment 05 independently contributed [M-Track](../evidence/05/code_audit.md), [NavNuances](../evidence/05/navnuances_code_audit.md), [ULN](../evidence/05/uln_code_audit.md) and [M³E](../evidence/05/m3e_original_audit.md) original-source audits. Its compile/AST-only M-Track and M³E checks are separately attributed and did not execute source modules. Material findings include:

- SAME's pinned router normalizes over the batch axis, making batch-one routing state-independent before top-k selection.
- M³E's release evaluates all experts, counts micro-only workloads, and has compile/wiring/shape blockers; its cross-stage BWT/FWT evaluator is not present in the inspected paths.
- slow4fast-VLN's documented agent does not integrate the reflection modules, whose LLM/text embedding routines contain explicit placeholders.
- ULN's released uncertainty input uses attended features rather than the paper's score concatenation; its agreement target follows stored rollout paths, and branch scoring uses raw logits.
- Explore until Confident releases semantic exploration and answer aggregation, but the inspected tree did not expose its conformal stopping path.
- UoT's nested expected-reward routines both multiply branch probabilities, requiring clarification before claiming paper-equivalent expected information gain.

These identify reproducibility limits at exact commits. None establishes that the authors used those same files to produce the published results, and none is an accusation of fabricated results.

## Implications for the proposal evaluations

The strongest opportunities are measurable distinctions rather than another generic verifier or memory module: independently validated clause/instance evidence; separation of language validity from navigator difficulty; ordered future events that preserve route obligations; and failure memory that separates identity from controller capability. Assignment 07's proposals and the independent evaluators should test these distinctions against the established grounding, recovery, calibration and adaptation mechanisms above. A small controlled experiment should hold navigation backbone, training data, feedback budget, action budget and evaluator fixed, and expose cases in which endpoint success, valid language and physically achieved obligations disagree.

Original-paper bottleneck labels here are scoped initial assessments. Current follow-up resolution belongs to [assignment 05](05_summary.md) and [assignment 06](06_summary.md), whose matrices should be joined by bottleneck and setting rather than publication date or headline SR. In particular, a later benchmark improvement does not automatically resolve an earlier semantic or causal ambiguity.
