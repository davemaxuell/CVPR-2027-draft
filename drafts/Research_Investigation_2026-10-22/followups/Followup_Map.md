# Follow-up map

**Cutoff: 22 October 2026.** Arrows below mean a problem/mechanism relationship in this investigation, not necessarily an author citation or direct code lineage. The [57-row matrix](Bottleneck_Matrix.csv) records the exact setting, evidence, comparability, resolution status and remaining gap. Original-paper limitations also have their own [207 structured records](../evidence/paper_bottlenecks.json); the follow-up matrix prioritizes shared and proposal-relevant questions rather than asserting a later solution for every record.

| Research chain | What later work establishes within its setting | What remains to test | Matrix rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speaker-Follower / RCM → compatibility and human instruction evaluation → ScaleVLN / SRDF / RSI-Nav | Generation and navigator-based selection can improve navigation; human wayfinding and compatibility are established quality signals. | Whether a fixed selector admits invalid easy pairs or rejects valid hard pairs, using independent semantic labels and equal accepted data. | 05-D01–D09 |
| FGR2R / GELA / Landmark-RxR / M-TRACK → CorrectNav / BudVLN / SeekVLN | Chunk alignment, milestone checks, recovery supervision and information-seeking already exist. | Whether teacher labels depend correctly on unfinished obligations at matched pose, with valid alternative routes and equal disturbances. | 05-R01–R08, R10–R11 |

The matrix distinguishes **resolved in a specified setting**, **partially addressed**, **shifted into another cost**, **unresolved in the reviewed scope**, and **uncertain**. For example, SC²-WM resolves the narrow premise that no observed residual update exists; it does not establish calibrated decision risk. Landmark-RxR supplies local-skill measurement under oracle resets; it does not solve autonomous recovery. A higher aggregate success rate alone is never used here as a resolution certificate.
