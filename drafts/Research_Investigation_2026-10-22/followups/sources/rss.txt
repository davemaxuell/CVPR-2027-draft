variants. These examples demonstrate how differ-
ent rewriting strategies introduce increasing lev-

semantic indirection while strictly preserving the

reformulates instructions by emphasizing implicit
reasoning or final-state constraints, and R4 explic-
itly injects object-level distractors via negation.
