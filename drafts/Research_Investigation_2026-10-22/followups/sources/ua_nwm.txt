                         Abstract
                                                                                       Goal Position

          Aerial image-goal navigation requires an unmanned aerial
           vehicle (UAV) to reach a target location specified by a goal
          image.  Existing world-model-based methods rank candi-
          date trajectories using predicted futures, but typically rely

           future-state uncertainty. To address this limitation, we pro-         Context States     Future Actions          Future State

       NWM), an efficient latent world model for aerial image-Aug

           sents plausible futures with an uncertainty subspace and de-                              ?
