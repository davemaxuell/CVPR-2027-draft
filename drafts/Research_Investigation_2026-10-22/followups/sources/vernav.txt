                            Abstract

          Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) requires an agent
           to navigate through unseen 3D environments according to
           natural-language instructions. Explicit reasoning can improve2026
           instruction understanding and semantic grounding, but autore-
           gressive generation at every step accumulates large decision-
           stage latency over multi-step navigation. We propose VerNav,Sep     a verifier-first framework for low-latency LLM-based VLN.
1    The verifier reduces decision-stage latency by replacing per-
           step autoregressive generation with batched action verifica-
            tion, while an entropy-based adaptive generator is invoked
          only for uncertain decisions to produce compact state evi-
          dence. To further improve navigation performance with the
             verifier, we introduce a two-stage alignment scheme: (i) VPO
          improves local action-preference alignment in static verifier
            training, and (ii) step-level reinforcement fine-tuning provides[cs.RO]          dense progress rewards over multi-step navigation rollouts
          during dynamic task execution. Experiments on the Room-to-
