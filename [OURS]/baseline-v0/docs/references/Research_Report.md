# Vision-and-language navigation: evidence and research directions

**Literature cutoff: 22 October 2026. Status: complete within documented evidence and access limits.**

The strongest next step is a **recovery-label audit before new model training**. This investigation supports three conditional projects: recovery labels, route-validity evidence for data selection, and claim-specific negative memory updates. It does **not** support calling their generic mechanisms new. The independent novelty search found direct predecessors for language-aligned teachers, history-conditioned task-state estimation, validity-plus-difficulty filtering, and failure attribution before memory revision. The fourth draft, ordered future-event prediction, is rejected as an original-method proposal because generalized value functions and predictive-state representations already express that type of question. [Ranked recommendation](proposals/Final_Recommendation.md), [independent novelty review](evaluations/09_novelty.md).

The package covers **131 papers and one internal report, represented by 157 supplied PDFs**. Every entry has a dossier with method, training/inference, experiments, implementation scope and specific limitations. Four original-paper assignments each covered 33 entries; two assignments traced follow-ups; one synthesized proposals; three independently challenged logic, novelty and feasibility. The ten-assignment ledger records the work and subsequent revisions. Unavailable code, unread videos and limited figure inspection are stated rather than counted as completed reviews. [Dossier index](inventory/Dossier_Index.md), [file inventory](inventory/all_files.csv), [assignment ledger](Assignment_Ledger.md), [investigation method](appendices/Investigation_Method.md).

## What the research landscape establishes

The useful distinctions are mechanisms and information contracts, not mutually exclusive paper families. A DUET-derived navigator can use a VLM, synthetic data, a world model and persistent memory simultaneously. Continuous environments and cross-entropy loss are different axes. Training-free inference still relies on pretrained components and can include outcome-labeled memory updates. The existing folder groups support discovery; the dossiers and comparison rules support scientific comparisons. [Ten-dimension comparison guide](appendices/Comparison_Guide.md), [folder browser](../Paper/Paper_Groups.html).

Several tempting broad premises do not survive inspection. DUET can backtrack; separate STOP parameterization is not automatically a new probability model; SRDF's appendix includes grounding evaluation; SC²-WM already uses observed prediction residuals; WMNav has a goal-cell override; and there is no controlled evidence here that synthetic scaling necessarily makes agents ignore language. The [earlier-idea audit](appendices/Previous_Idea_Claim_Audit.md) records the relevant corrections. EOE is the user-confirmed ICML 2024 image-OOD paper: its generated negative labels are relevant adjacent prior art, but its score does not establish navigation-action risk. [EOE dossier and supplementary review](dossiers/eoe.md).

## Which bottlenecks remain

The [follow-up map](followups/Followup_Map.md) and **57-row resolution matrix** connect original limitations, subsequent mechanisms, evidence, comparability and remaining gaps. They distinguish resolved within a specified setting, partially addressed, shifted into another cost, unresolved within the reviewed scope, and uncertain. Higher success alone is not treated as resolution. The individual dossiers retain additional paper-specific limitations. [Matrix](followups/Bottleneck_Matrix.csv), [structured original bottlenecks](evidence/paper_bottlenecks.json).

Three recurring distinctions motivate the retained projects:

1. **Endpoint success, efficient recovery and instruction completion differ.** A geometric teacher may efficiently reach the goal while omitting a genuinely required event; conversely, a longer route can still obey the instruction. A first-action mismatch is therefore insufficient to label a semantic failure. The audit must examine independently validated requirements and complete teacher continuations, including STOP. Existing LAW, milestone and reward-machine methods are mandatory controls. [P1 logic analysis](evaluations/08_logic.md), [recovery follow-ups](followups/05_review.md).
2. **Navigator competence and route-language validity differ.** A successful rollout is not an independent truth label for every clause, and a valid difficult pair may be rejected by a weak navigator. Compatibility, human instruction evaluation, SRDF/RSI-Nav and CVS already address nearby selection problems. The remaining candidate is a fixed-pool, independently adjudicated route-witness study under equal data, annotation effort and training exposure. [Data-selection follow-ups](followups/05_review.md), [P2 independent analysis](evaluations/08_logic.md).
3. **An episode failure does not identify which stored claim is false.** Recognition, control and completion can fail separately. Generic diagnosis and memory revision already exist. A narrower question is whether deployable evidence can prevent harmful negative semantic writes beyond simply freezing or quarantining failure updates, under the same representation, feedback and memory budget. The inspected PSG release supplies a conditional mechanism to test, not a measured natural failure rate. [PSG-Nav source extension](dossiers/psgnav.md), [P4 feasibility analysis](evaluations/10_feasibility.md).

Other important gaps concern acquisition cost, visibility-conditioned absence decisions, calibration of the actual decision event, and controller/representation confounds. CLOSER, SeekVLN, VLN-NF, KnowNo, Explore until Confident and recent world-model/calibration work are strong predecessors. These areas need precise interventions rather than another generic verifier or uncertainty score. [Prioritized gap definitions and success criteria](proposals/Research_Gaps.md), [follow-up sources and access scope](followups/Sources.md).

## Proposed projects and independent revisions

| Priority | Retained project | Candidate contribution | First decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **P1: Recovery labels** | An alternative-aware, independently validated recovery-label protocol and a matched teacher intervention. Established task-state models are baselines. | Audit natural teacher continuations and controlled paired histories; stop if ambiguity, harmless detours or a strong existing teacher explain the gap. |
| 2 | **P2: Route validity** | Route-clause witness evidence and a controlled selection study beyond generic validity-plus-hardness filtering. | Audit one fixed pool, including unresolved examples; compare equal-acceptance and equal-human-effort selection before training. |
| 3 | **P4: Negative memory** | Claim-specific negative-write attribution with deployable evidence, if it improves on conservative memory policies. | Measure natural harmful writes and later suppression; compare freeze/quarantine, witness-only and attribution with retrieval fixed. |

The [revised proposal document](proposals/final_proposals.md) specifies hypotheses, variables, objectives, algorithms, training/inference changes, costs, starting commits/files, experiments, ablations, falsification and risks. These are **conditional research studies**; stronger method claims require new evidence. No empirical navigation benefit is established for any retained project.

The independent evaluations materially changed the drafts. P1 now separates semantics from efficiency and includes the action-menu, termination and return contract. P2 distinguishes alternative witnesses from jointly necessary evidence and exposes selectively missing labels. P4 treats the learned output as an unvalidated score unless probability assumptions are justified and evaluates the entire correlated witness gate. Its released RAG implementation is separated from paper EEC. P3 is removed from the method shortlist despite a repaired and valid symbolic counterexample. ## Executed evidence and its limits

The team executed original finite checks, static dataset analysis and carefully isolated source functions. **No trained navigation benchmark, model training, simulator rollout or physical robot experiment ran.** The [experiment register](experiments/README.md) contains setups, scripts, inputs/hashes, results and explicit limits.

The static geometry audit found proper prefixes with full SPL in 4,051 of 4,675 local R2R training paths and 712 of 783 validation-unseen paths under the inspected evaluator's tolerance. This is expected metric behavior. No navigator selected these prefixes, and the analysis did not label any instruction violated. It motivates measuring semantics separately; it is not an SRDF error rate. [Geometry results](experiments/geometry/results.json).

Independent AST-extracted source tests confirmed SkillNav batch/order-dependent expert dispatch with fake expert functions, and a conditional PSG negative-write/read pathway using synthetic crops and explicit stubs. These are release-contract findings, not reproductions of the published systems. UAGM's Table 11 p-values also fail a five-run paired-t consistency bound under the caption's straightforward interpretation, allowing rounding. A different statistical unit or protocol requires clarification; no misconduct or invalidation of mean gains is inferred. [Source checks](experiments/10/source_check_results.json), [mathematical checks and assumptions](experiments/08/logic_results.json).

The current machine has an 8 GB-class RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, but installed PyTorch is CPU-only and navigation simulators are absent. Local R2R annotations and 72 connectivity graphs are available; the raw annotations lack the instruction encodings expected by the selected DUET loader. Usable visual features, licensed scenes and a compatible navigator checkpoint remain unestablished. Remote checkpoint listings were inspected, but weights were not downloaded or evaluated. [Independent resource audit](evaluations/10_feasibility.md).

## Practical starting point and package guide

Begin with [the recovery audit protocol](experiments/Initial_Experiment_Plan.md) and [empty annotation template](experiments/recovery_annotation_template.csv). The suggested 200 natural checkpoints and 100 controlled pairs are proposed pilot sizes, not collected data or a power calculation. Secure visual traces and a verified frozen policy first. Use a common primitive-neighbor-plus-STOP interface for the first oracle diagnostic; handle map-dependent macro actions and cached return explicitly when integrating native DUET. The already examined validation geometry is development evidence, so preserve a separate untouched final test.

The recommendation changes if P1's natural gap is negligible, P2 shows a clear witness-based improvement beyond strong selectors, or P4 reveals consequential harm that quarantine cannot address. The [ranked recommendation](proposals/Final_Recommendation.md) states those gates and remaining objections. A null result at an early gate is a reason to stop or reframe, not to add model complexity.
